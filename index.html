Research

Selected work

Constraining a Korean CTC decoder

For my master’s thesis, I combined token, lexicon, and grammar transducers into one search graph for pretrained Citrinet and Conformer models. The grammar reduced errors on loanwords but increased errors on spontaneous speech outside its vocabulary. Whether to use it depended on what speakers were free to say.

Recognizing speech across channels

At Selvas AI, I adapted speech recognizers to 8 kHz telephone and radio recordings. I compared gains on the target channel with changes on held-out general speech, then examined errors by dialect and noise condition before selecting a model.

Synthetic speech for ASR training

I generated more than 4,000 hours of training speech from text for command recognition and other ASR tasks. To evaluate whether the synthetic examples helped, I tracked recognition accuracy on the target vocabulary and regressions on general speech.

Measuring atypical speech

At Sogang, I annotated therapist–patient recordings and used phoneme recognition to estimate clinical speech measures. The estimates correlated with human ratings at 0.94–0.97. Predicting a patient’s severity stage was a separate, harder task, with 74.85% accuracy.

Proposed doctoral research

Audio deepfake detection

Does a telephone channel make genuine speech look synthetic?

I want to pass the same genuine and synthesized utterances through matched telephone conditions, then measure how often a detector changes its decision. At a threshold fixed on development data, I would report false alarms on genuine speakers and missed detections on unseen generators, broken down by language and channel. Paired recordings would let me isolate the effect of transmission from differences in what was said.
